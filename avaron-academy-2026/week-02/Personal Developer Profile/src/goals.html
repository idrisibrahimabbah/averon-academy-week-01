My Goals as a Beginner in Full-Stack Software Development

Short Term Goals

  • Strengthen My Fundamentals: Improve my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and GitHub by practising regularly.
  • Build Practical Projects: Develop at least three simple web applications to gain hands-on experience and build my portfolio.
  • Learn Backend Development: Acquire basic skills in Node.js, Express.js, databases, and APIs to understand how frontend and backend systems work together.

Long Term Goal

My Long Term Goal is to:

Become a Professional Full-Stack Developer: Develop the skills and experience needed to build, deploy, and maintain complete web applications and secure a rewarding career in software development.
